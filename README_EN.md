# Digital Millennium / 数字千年计划

🌐 [中文](README.md)

## 🧭 What This Is

Digital Millennium is a long-term open effort to turn practical human wisdom into capabilities that people can understand and AI can use.

That wisdom can come from experience, books, research, standards, and working methods. The project is not simply collecting material. It is concerned with how knowledge is extracted, how its sources and formation are traced, how real use produces feedback, and how the resulting capabilities are revised over time.

This repository is infrastructure for that work. It is not another Skill warehouse or a Skill download site. A Skill is one possible output of the ongoing transformation process, not the project itself.

## 🧩 How a Skill Takes Shape

A Skill may begin with an experience, a book, a study, or a method. It is then extracted and organized, with its source and development kept visible. Real use provides feedback, and that feedback informs revision and further evolution.

The protocol is still taking shape. Nothing here should be treated as a finalized standard. The practical test is whether a capability remains understandable to people, usable by AI, and open to learning from experience.

## 🌱 The Early Stage

The project is currently establishing its first reference Skill and refining the standard through real use and feedback.

Early review will stay lightweight. It will focus on the specification, format, and declaration of sources, rather than introducing complex committees or voting. The rules can change as actual contributions and experience reveal what works.

People from different professions, educational paths, and life backgrounds are welcome to watch, discuss, or suggest ideas. There is no finalized contribution workflow to present yet.

## 🗺️ A Possible Future Matrix

As the body of work grows, the project may use an extensible matrix to connect Skills. One dimension could describe application domains, while another describes cognitive and action capabilities such as understanding, judgment, planning, or execution. This is a direction for exploration, not a completed taxonomy.

## 👀 Follow Along

You can watch the project take shape, join the discussion, or suggest a piece of practical wisdom worth developing. The rules will be refined through use and feedback, rather than written in full before the work begins.
